About the Program

Beauty is a serious business frontier.

BeautyTech U™ is a next-generation workforce development and innovation program that uses the beauty industry as a lens to teach the most in-demand skills of the modern economy — AI, data, systems thinking, consumer intelligence, entrepreneurship, and cultural storytelling.

Designed for students and professionals across disciplines — business, computer science, communications, marketing, and entrepreneurship — BTU meets learners where their curiosity already lives.

Presented by Ebony J. Karim, Founder & CEO of The Beauty Genie® — as seen in Forbes, ESSENCE, and Black Enterprise.

Four Curriculum Tracks
Track 01
Business & Brand Strategy
Entrepreneurship, brand-building, funding, and innovation strategy.
Track 02
Technology & Innovation
AI, machine learning, personalization, creator economy, and social commerce.
Track 03
Cosmetic Chemistry
Formulation science, ingredient knowledge, and product development pipelines.
Track 04
Beauty Retail & Consumer Experience
Retail operations, brand-to-shelf strategy, and multicultural consumer trends.

The Capstone
The BTU Blueprint™

Every student completes a capstone project demonstrating their ability to apply learning across all four curriculum tracks — then presents live to an industry panel on Pitch Day. A score of 70/100 or above earns the BeautyTech U™ Certificate of Completion.

Track A — Open Brief
Develop an original product or service concept. You define the problem, the consumer, and the solution — start to finish.
Track B — Brand Challenge™
A partner brand submits a real business problem. You solve it. Evaluated by brand executives — the same people who make hiring decisions.
